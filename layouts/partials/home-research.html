Research overview

AIGC Authenticity & Robustness

Question: How can generated video be identified from stable temporal evidence instead of shortcuts such as watermarks, codecs, or generator identity?

Current focus: Representation quality, cross-frame consistency, numerical stability, and robustness under compression and post-processing.

Latent Representations & Generative Dynamics

Question: What structure is preserved in a generative model's latent space, and how does data move through that space?

Current focus: Tokenizers, continuous representations, editing trajectories, optimal transport, and signals that remain stable across models.

View direction

3D Vision & Human Reconstruction

Question: How can limited 2D observations recover an editable, drivable, and identity-consistent 3D human representation?

Current focus: Monocular capture, 3DGS pipelines, geometry priors, identity-expression separation, and dynamic head modeling.
